Music Sheets Hub 1.6.138

This update improves guitar tab timing, technique marks, and supported tunings.

Windows 64-bit. Extract the whole ZIP, then open Music Sheets Sync Hub.exe.
Keep MusicSheetsNativePage beside the EXE. It handles desktop sheet processing.

In Music Sheets, open Syncing > Library Sync and enable syncing.
Choose Pair with a friend using a code and enter the same generated code on your devices and in the Hub.
Google linking needs a separately configured desktop OAuth client. Once configured, link the same Google account on your devices and in the Hub.
In the Hub, choose Save & start syncing.
Keep your PC and devices on the same local network.

Closing the window keeps the Hub running in the system tray.
To update, exit the old Hub and replace the EXE and worker folder together.
Keep your existing library and settings.

Third-party notices are included under Notices and in the worker runtime.
